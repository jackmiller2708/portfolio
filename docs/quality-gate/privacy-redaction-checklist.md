# Privacy Redaction Checklist

Use this checklist before publishing or expanding case studies.

## Required Checks

- [ ] Redaction status is set to `public`, `redacted`, or `synthetic`.
- [ ] No client, employer, vendor, or team names appear without permission.
- [ ] No private architecture diagrams or screenshots are included.
- [ ] No internal repository names, paths, ticket IDs, or incident IDs are included.
- [ ] No proprietary metrics are presented as public facts.
- [ ] Production incidents are generalized so they cannot identify a client or employer.
- [ ] Evidence is either synthetic, redacted, or described at a safe level of abstraction.
- [ ] Recommendations avoid exposing private strategy or roadmap details.
